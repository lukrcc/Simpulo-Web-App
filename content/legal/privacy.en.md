Simpulo is private, not anonymous: the people you live with see your first name and face – nothing public you didn't choose.

## Who is responsible {#controller}

{{company.name}}, {{company.street}}, {{company.city}}, {{company.country}}. Privacy requests: {{company.privacyEmail}}.

## What we collect {#data}

- **Account:** first name, email, sign-in method, date of birth (to check you're {{age.min}} or older – others only ever see your age, and only if you turn it on).
- **Profile:** photos, a short text, languages, social links you add yourself, your country.
- **Houses and requests:** the Houses you open, request, join or save, and your notes to hosts.
- **Messages:** your chats in Simpulo.
- **Payments:** what was paid, when, refunds. Card details stay with the payment provider – Simpulo never sees them.
- **Technical data:** device, browser, logs needed to run the service.

## What we never collect {#never}

No data about religion, sexual orientation, health, political opinions or origin. We don't analyse what you write in chats – a report only carries its category and the excerpt you report.

## Why we use it {#purposes}

To run Simpulo: show Houses, pass requests to hosts, process payments, send the notices about money and deadlines, keep people safe and handle reports. Legal bases per purpose are still to be added.

## Who sees what {#sharing}

- Your **host and crew** see your full profile once you're in the same House.
- **Signed-in members** see your first name and photo in a crew – unless you turn off "Show me on House pages".
- **Logged out**, nobody sees guests' names or photos, only the number in a crew.
- Your last name is never shown to others.
- Guest profiles are never indexed by search engines; a host page only if the host turns it on.
- Service providers (hosting, payments, email) – the list is still to be added.

## How long we keep it {#retention}

Account data until you delete your account. Retention periods per data type (for example payment records under tax law) are still to be added.

## Your choices {#choices}

In Settings › Privacy you decide whether you show on House pages, whether your age shows and whether your photo stays hidden until a host accepts you. You can pause your account (private for everyone, nothing deleted), download your data or delete your account.

## Your rights {#rights}

You can ask for access, correction, deletion, restriction and a copy of your data, object to processing and complain to a supervisory authority. Write to {{company.privacyEmail}}.

## Cookies {#cookies}

Only what's needed to run Simpulo – see [Cookies](/cookies).
