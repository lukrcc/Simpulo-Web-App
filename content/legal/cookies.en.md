No cookie banner – we only store what's needed to make Simpulo work.

No advertising, no tracking across sites, no analytics cookies. If that ever changes, we ask you first.

## Cookies {#cookies}

| Name | What it's for | How long |
| --- | --- | --- |
| simpulo_theme | Light, dark or system – so the first screen is right. | {{cookies.themeDays}} days |
| viewer_country | Only your country code, to show prices in your currency. | {{cookies.countryDays}} days |
| simpulo-locale | Your language, once Simpulo speaks more than one. | Until you change it |
| simpulo_preview | Before the launch: that you entered the preview password. Never the password itself. | {{cookies.previewDays}} days |

## On your device {#storage}

Your browser keeps these on your device. They never leave it unless you send something yourself.

| Name | What it's for | How long |
| --- | --- | --- |
| simpulo:currency | The currency you picked. | Until you clear it |
| simpulo:currency-hint | That we already told you which currency prices are in. | Until you clear it |
| simpulo:house-photo-hint | That we already showed you, once, that a House's photo slides to the next one. | Until you clear it |
| simpulo:install-prompt:v2 | When we last suggested the app, and "Don't show again". | Until you clear it |
| simpulo:push-asked | When we asked about notifications – at most twice. | Until you clear it |
| simpulo:install-seen:{id} | Which of your bookings already showed the app hint, so it doesn't repeat. | Until you clear it |
| simpulo:standalone-welcomed | That the installed app already welcomed you. | Until you clear it |
| draftHouse | Your unfinished House in Open a House. | Until you open or delete it |
| simpulo:house-templates | Houses you saved as a template. | Until you delete them |
| simpulo:idea-votes | Ideas you voted for. | Until you clear it |
| simpulo:admin:period | Team only: the last time range picked in Admin. | Until you clear it |

## Until you close the tab {#session}

| Name | What it's for | How long |
| --- | --- | --- |
| simpulo:draft:{chat} | A message you started but didn't send yet. | Until you close the tab |
| simpulo:spotlight-intro | The Spotlight's intro plays once, not on every page. | Until you close the tab |
| simpulo:hero | Which Houses the Spotlight already showed you, so it shows new ones. | Until you close the tab |
| simpulo:in-app-hint-dismissed | That you closed the "Open in your browser" hint. | Until you close the tab |

## Files you add {#files}

| Name | What it's for | How long |
| --- | --- | --- |
| simpulo-memories | Photos and clips you add to a House album, until they're stored online for your crew. | Until you remove them |

## Statistics without cookies {#statistics}

If we count how Simpulo is used, we do it without cookies: nothing is stored on your device, nothing links one visit to the next, and no profile of you is built. It runs on servers in the EU. Today it's switched off.

## Videos and maps {#embeds}

Videos from other sites load only when you tap play. Maps are pictures made on our server – your browser doesn't contact a map service until you open directions yourself.

## Your choice {#choice}

You can block or delete all of this in your browser. Simpulo still works; the theme and currency fall back to the defaults. More on your data: [Privacy](/privacy).
