Simple rules, the same for every House.

## Before the crew is complete {#beforeComplete}

Withdraw your request or leave your spot any time, for free. Nothing is paid before the crew is complete and the place is chosen.

## After you paid {#afterPayment}

You can leave by handing your spot to the next person on the waitlist. Once they take it, you get everything back except the Simpulo fee.

## Nobody on the waitlist {#withoutTransfer}

If you leave after paying and nobody takes your spot, there's no refund – the crew has booked the place counting on you.

## The host cancels or doesn't book {#hostCancels}

If the host cancels the House, or doesn't confirm the booking within {{payment.bookingHours}} hours after everyone paid, you get your whole share back.

## Unpaid spots {#unpaid}

Once your share is due you have {{payment.windowHours}} hours to pay. After that your spot is free again and nothing is charged.

## Refunds {#refunds}

Refunds go back to the way you paid. Your bank usually needs 5–10 days.
