A House works when everyone can feel at home. These guidelines apply in every House, chat and profile.

## Be yourself {#real}

Your real first name, your own photos, honest words about you and your House. One account per person.

## Respect everyone {#respect}

No harassment, threats, hate or pressure – in person or in chats. No means no. Harassment means you leave the House.

## Look after the place {#place}

Treat the place like your own: leave it as you found it, and tell the host when something breaks.

## Keep the House agreement {#agreement}

Each crew agrees on quiet hours, cooking and friends over. Stick to what your crew agreed, and to the host's House rules.

## Respect privacy {#privacy}

What happens in the House stays in the crew. Only share photos of others in the House album when everyone in them is okay with it – and remove any photo when someone asks.

## Keep money in Simpulo {#money}

Pay and get paid only through Simpulo. Don't sell courses or products to your crew, and don't use a House to advertise.

## For hosts {#hosts}

As a host you set the tone. On top of everything above:

- Describe your House honestly – the place, the dates, the price.
- Answer every request, yes or no.
- Book the place once everyone has paid, and confirm the booking within {{payment.bookingHours}} hours.
- Hosting where you live: make sure you're allowed to have guests.

## Stay safe {#safety}

If something feels wrong, report it in one tap. In an emergency, call the local emergency number first.

## When rules are broken {#consequences}

We look at every report. Depending on what happened, we warn the person, remove them from the House or suspend their account. Harassment in a House means leaving the House. Both sides hear what we decided.
