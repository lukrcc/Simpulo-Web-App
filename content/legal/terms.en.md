These terms apply to everyone who uses Simpulo – hosts and guests alike.

## What Simpulo is {#role}

Simpulo connects people who want to share a place for a while. Simpulo is not an accommodation provider and does not sell travel: the host books the place, Simpulo handles requests, chats and payments between the crew.

## Who can use it {#who}

You must be {{age.min}} or older. One account per person, with your real first name and photo.

## Requests {#requests}

- A request is free. The host says yes or no – nobody gets in without the host's yes.
- A request the host doesn't answer runs out after {{request.expiryHours}} hours.
- A House can be for a group (for example women only); the host decides.

## Paying {#payments}

- Everyone in the crew – the host too – pays the same share of the place.
- Guests add Simpulo's service fee of {{fees.service}} % as its own line. The host pays no service fee.
- Nothing is paid before the crew is complete and the place is chosen. Then each guest has {{payment.windowHours}} hours to pay; after that the spot is free again.
- A host may add a host fee per guest for leading the House (coaching, a programme, guiding), shown to guests as its own line. Simpulo keeps {{fees.hostFee}} % of it.
- Payments run through our payment provider, in the House's currency.

## Hosts {#hosts}

Hosts describe their House honestly, open only Houses they mean to book, book the place once everyone has paid and confirm the booking within {{payment.bookingHours}} hours – otherwise everyone gets their share back. Hosts are responsible for the local rules on short stays and, when hosting where they live, for being allowed to have guests.

## Cancellation {#cancellation}

The rules are on [Cancellation](/cancellation): free until the crew is complete; after paying only by handing the spot to the waitlist; everything back if the host cancels or doesn't book.

## Living together {#conduct}

Everyone follows the [Community guidelines](/community-guidelines). Selling courses or products to the crew, commercial listings disguised as Houses, and illegal, hateful or misleading content are not allowed.

## Reports and accounts {#safety}

You can report and block people, Houses, messages and photos. Simpulo looks at every report and can warn a person, remove them from a House or suspend an account. In an emergency, call the local emergency number first.

## Leaving Simpulo {#deletion}

You can pause or delete your account in Settings at any time. Open payments, running Houses and legal retention duties can delay the deletion of some data.

## Liability and law {#law}

Liability, the applicable law and the place of jurisdiction are still to be written with a lawyer.
