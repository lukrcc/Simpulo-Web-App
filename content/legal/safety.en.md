How we help you live with people you can trust.

## Real profiles {#safety-profiles}

Real first names and faces. Anyone can verify their ID with Stripe Identity – verified people carry a badge. Your last name is never shown.

## Hosts choose who joins {#safety-requests}

Every request needs the host's yes. Hosts can ask for verified guests only. You see who's in before you pay.

## Paying safely {#safety-payments}

You pay through Simpulo, only after the host said yes and the crew is complete. If the host doesn't confirm the booking within {{payment.bookingHours}} hours, everyone gets their share back. Never pay outside Simpulo.

## At the House {#safety-onSite}

From {{help.daysBefore}} days before until {{help.daysAfter}} days after the House, "Something went wrong" is right in your House. In an emergency, call the local emergency number first – Simpulo is not an emergency service.

## For hosts {#safety-hosts}

You decide who joins, the money comes through Simpulo once everyone has paid, and you can get help during the stay. Hosting where you live: check that you're allowed to have guests.

## Reporting a problem {#safety-reporting}

Report a person, a House, a message or a photo in one tap. We look at every report within {{report.reviewHours}} hours; the person doesn't learn who reported them. You can block someone at the same time. See also the [Community guidelines](/community-guidelines).
